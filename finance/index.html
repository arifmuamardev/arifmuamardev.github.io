Unclassified transactions (Others)

Items lacking enough evidence for a more precise category. Click to open the transaction, review original description and receipts, then choose a category yourself. No automatic rewriting.

Balance checkpoints to review

JPY-only posted records with recorded balances. We compare consecutive balance checkpoints with intervening JPY income and expenses. Account transfers and incomplete account scope can create legitimate differences. Largest gaps first.

Possible duplicate records

Same date, currency, amounts, item, category, place, payment type, note, conversion amount, and running balance. Identical purchases may still be legitimate. Click to inspect one record.

IDR conversion exceptions

Checks stored IDR equivalents against the dataset's typical historical rate; rounding tolerance is at least Rp1 or 2%. Non-matching values may use a valid different rate. JPY-origin expenses are not tested with this rule.

Interpretation and next steps

Source currency is retained separately from the normalized JPY amount. IDR-origin records normally lack a JPY running-balance checkpoint, so the JPY-balance test intentionally excludes them.

These checks cannot establish bank reconciliation, missing off-ledger transactions, or whether two similar purchases truly occurred. Verify against bank statements and receipts before editing an entry. All checks run locally on data already loaded after authentication.

Scope: Read-only, no database writes, no automated deduplication, no changes to authentication or account balances. The latest running balance is not recomputed or overwritten by this diagnostic.

Account Evidence & Transfer Review

View each account's last recorded statement snapshot separately. Indonesian bank and wallet balances are denominated in IDR; their stored JPY equivalents are informational and must not be added to the Japanese cash ledger. Nothing on this page adjusts a balance.

Latest JPY ledger balance—Not a per-bank balance
Indonesian statements—Separate IDR accounts
IDR balance differences—Investigate, not errors proved
ATM activity—Movement records, not balance
JPY transfer-related markers—Not paired to accounts
Do not add these balances together. The JPY ledger balance may already reflect money movements involving the Indonesian accounts, ATM withdrawals and card settlements. JP Post Bank, Hokuriku Bank, and physical cash do not have separately synchronized statement balances here. Recorded balances are historic snapshots, not real-time bank balances.

Japan · requires manually verified balances

The existing Reconciliation form asks for all three accounts. No reliable account-by-account current balances are available from the linked transaction tables. Review actual account statements before recording any adjustment.

Indonesia · independent account statements

Source IDR balances and stored approximate yen equivalents are shown as recorded at each statement's latest date. Recency differs by account; values are never treated as current or summed with the JPY ledger.

Native IDR statement continuity

For each bank/wallet, compare the change between consecutive recorded balances against signed statement amounts, with Rp1 rounding tolerance. Gaps could reflect missing imports, duplicate ledger rows, reversed order, or genuine reporting differences.

JPY ledger movement markers

Entries labeled internal transfer, bank inflow, remittance, fees, or balance reconciliation. They are not automatically matched to ATM or IDR statements, and are not additional expenses merely because money moved.

What this can and cannot establish

This view gives source-separated evidence and balance continuity checks. It does not connect Japan Post Bank or Hokuriku accounts to ledger rows, establish which ATM source funds which destination, convert current balances using live FX, or prove that missing statement lines are fraudulent. Compare records and receipts before making a manual reconciliation adjustment.

All calculations run locally from existing authenticated data; no new Neon tables or API write operations are introduced.
